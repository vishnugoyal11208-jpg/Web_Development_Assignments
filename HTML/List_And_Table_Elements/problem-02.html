The Royal Spice Garden

Welcome to The Royal Spice Garden, a family-friendly restaurant offering a delicious combination of traditional Indian flavors and modern culinary styles. Our chefs prepare every dish using fresh ingredients, aromatic spices, and carefully selected recipes. Whether you are looking for a quick snack, a complete meal, a sweet dessert, or a refreshing beverage, our menu has something for everyone.

Starters

    Our starters are perfect for beginning your meal. They are freshly prepared and served with flavorful dips and sauces.
  • Paneer Tikka – Soft pieces of paneer marinated with yogurt and Indian spices, grilled with onions and capsicum. Chef's Special
  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with fresh vegetables and served with sweet chili sauce. Bestseller
  • Hara Bhara Kebab – Delicious green vegetable and spinach kebabs served with mint chutney. Vegan
  • Masala Papad - Crispy papad topped with onions, tomatoes, coriander, and mild spices. Vegan

Main Course

Our main-course dishes are prepared with authentic spices and served with a variety of breads and rice options
  • Royal Shahi Paneer – Creamy paneer cooked in a rich tomato and cashew gravy. Chef's Special
  • Vegetable Biryani – Fragrant basmati rice cooked with fresh vegetables, herbs, and aromatic spices. Bestseller
  • Dal Makhani – Slow-cooked black lentils prepared with butter, cream, and traditional spices.
  • Tandoori Roti - Fresh whole-wheat Indian bread baked in a traditional tandoor.
  • Butter Naan – Soft naan topped with butter and served hot.
  • Chole Bhature Combo - Spicy chickpea curry served with fluffy fried bhature. Combo

Desserts

Finish your meal with our selection of traditional and modern desserts.
  • Gulab Jamun – Soft milk-solid dumplings soaked in warm sugar syrup.
  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served with vanilla ice cream. Bestsellor
  • Kesar Kulfi – Traditional Indian frozen dessert flavored with saffron and cardamom.
  • Rasmalai –
    • Soft cottage-cheese dumplings served in sweet, chilled saffron milk.

Beverages

Refresh yourself with our selection of hot and cold beverages.
  • Fresh Mango Shake – A creamy and refreshing shake prepared with fresh mangoes.
  • Masala Chai – Traditional Indian tea prepared with milk, tea leaves, ginger, cardamom, and spices.
  • Fresh Lime Soda – Refreshing lime drink available in sweet or salty flavor.
  • Cold Coffee – Chilled coffee blended with milk and ice cream.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

For customers who want to enjoy a complete meal, our chef recommends following this sequence:
  • Masala Papad – Begin with a light and crispy appetizer.
  • Paneer Tikka – Enjoy our signature grilled starter.
  • Vegetable Biryani – Continue with our aromatic main course.
  • Dal Makhani with Butter Naan – Add a rich traditional combination to your meal.
  • Kesar Kulfi – Finish your dining experience with a refreshing dessert.
  • Fresh Lime Soda – End your meal with a refreshing beverage.

Food Labels/Terms

  • Vegan – Prepared without meat, dairy, eggs, or other animal-based ingredients.
  • Chef's Special – A dish specially recommended by our chef.
  • Gluten-Free – A food option prepared without gluten-containing ingredients.
  • Bestseller – One of the most popular choices among our customers.
  • Combo – A combination of multiple food items offered together.
