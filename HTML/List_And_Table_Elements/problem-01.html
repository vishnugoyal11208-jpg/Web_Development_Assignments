Eligibility

Candidates must meet the following criteria to qualify for admission:
  • Successful completion of Higher Secondary Education (12th Grade or equivalent) from a recognized board.
  • A minimum aggregate score of 50% in core subjects.

  • Mandatory Requirement: Applicants must clear the National Entrance Evaluation Test (NEET) or the Apex University Assessment.

Application Process

Application Deadline : October 15, 2026
  1. Register an account on the official university admission.
  2. Fill out personal, academic, and contact details in the online application form.
  3. Upload scanned copies of all required documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee through the secure payment gateway.
  5. Submit the application form and download the confirmation receipt.Track your application status and wait for the announcement of the merit list.

Required Documents

  • High School Diploma / 10th Grade Passing Certificate
  • Higher Secondary / 12th Grade Mark Sheet
  • Valid Government-issued Photo Identification (Passport, Driver's License, or National ID)
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (White background)
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard
  • Transfer Certificate or Migration Certificate from the previous institution
