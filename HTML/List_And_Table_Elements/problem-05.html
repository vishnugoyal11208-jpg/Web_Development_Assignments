Annual College Fest 2026

Fest Introduction

Welcome to the Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of creativity, talent, technology, sports, music, and culture. The fest brings together students from different departments to participate in exciting competitions, showcase their skills, and enjoy memorable campus activities.

The fest will feature technical competitions, cultural performances, individual and team events, entertainment activities, and exciting prizes. Students are encouraged to participate actively and make the most of this annual celebration.

Registration Process

Students who want to participate in the fest must complete their registration before the deadline.

REGISTRATION DEADLINE: 30 September 2026

  1. Visit the official Annual College Fest 2026 registration page.
  2. Choose the competition or event in which you want to participate.
  3. Enter your name, college details, contact information, and other required details.
  4. Select the participation type, such as individual or team.
  5. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save the registration confirmation for future reference.
  8. Report to the event venue at least 15 minutes before the scheduled time.

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report to the venue before the scheduled event time.
  • Participants must follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Students must maintain discipline throughout the fest .
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must take care of college property and event equipment
  • Teams must have the required number of members for their event.
  • Decisions of the event judges and coordinators will be considered final.
  • Students should keep the campus clean and use designated waste bins.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which participants from different eligible colleges or departments can participate according to the event rules.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants work together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition in which a participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a competition or fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing, managing, and supervising a particular event.
Final Round
The final stage of a competition in which selected participants compete for the winning positions.
