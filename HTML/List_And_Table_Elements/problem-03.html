Sequence Before a Practical Class

Students should follow the following sequence before attending a practical class:

  1. Read the practical experiment instructions.
  2. Understand the theory behind the experiment.
  3. Prepare the required program or procedure.
  4. Bring the practical notebook and required materials.
  5. Attend the practical class on time.
  6. Perform the experiment under faculty guidance.
  7. Record the results in the practical notebook.
  8. Submit the completed practical work for evaluation.

Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain the required attendance.
  • Students must arrive in class before the lecture begins.
  • Students should carry their identity card on campus.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts and subject topics to students.
Lab
A practical session where students apply theoretical concepts using computers, equipment, or software.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on problem solving, discussion, and clarification of difficult topics.
Internal Assessment
An evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations, quizzes, or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity in which students perform experiments, write programs, or apply concepts learned in lectures.
