Bank Accounts

A bank account helps you keep your money safe. You can save, deposit, withdraw and transfer money easily.

Savings Account: Best for students and regular savings.
Current Account: Mostly used for business transactions.
Fixed Deposit: Helps you earn interest on saved money.

Learn more about banking from Reserve Bank of India.

Debit Cards and Credit Cards

A debit card uses money directly from your bank account. A credit card lets you borrow money up to a fixed limit.

Use credit cards carefully. Always pay your bill on time to avoid extra charges & interest.

“Do not save what is left after spending, but spend what is left after saving.” — Warren Buffett

Loans and EMI

A loan is money borrowed from a bank. You need to repay the loan amount with interest in monthly payments called EMI.

Important: Read all loan terms before signing any document.

EMI = Principal + Interest
Example: ₹10,000 + ₹500 = ₹10,500

Personal Finance Tips

  • Save a part of your income every month.
  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Pay credit card bills on time.
  • Keep an emergency fund.
  • Do not share banking passwords.
  • Compare loan interest rates before borrowing.
  • Use online banking carefully.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a bank and account type.
  2. Fill the account opening form.
  3. Submit identity and address proof.
  4. Deposit the required minimum amount.
  5. Receive your account number and debit card.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The extra money charged by a bank on a loan.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid every month for a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed from the bank.
Tenure
The total time given to repay a loan.
