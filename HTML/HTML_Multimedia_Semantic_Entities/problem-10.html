Welcome to National Tech & Career Expo 2026

National Tech & Career Expo is a large event for students, graduates, job seekers and technology companies. Students can explore internships, placements, workshops, certifications & networking opportunities.

The expo is useful for students interested in Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Data Science, Cyber Security and Cloud Computing.

Entry is free for students & registration is required. Event date: 20 December 2026.

Organizer Message

Our aim is to connect talented students with leading companies. This expo will help students understand industry requirements and choose the right career path.

“Your future career starts with one opportunity, one conversation and one step forward.” — Expo Organizer

Important Notice

Note: Bring your college ID card, updated resume and notebook for workshop notes.

Students should arrive 30 minutes early. Entry closes at 4:00 PM   only registered participants can attend.

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Wear formal or decent clothes.
  • Research companies before visiting.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Carry a notebook and pen.
  • Practice a short self-introduction.

Career Expo Terms

Career Expo
An event where students meet companies and career experts.
Internship
Short-term work experience for students.
Placement
A job opportunity offered to eligible students.
Workshop
A practical learning session led by an expert.
Certification
A document showing successful completion of a course.
Networking
Meeting people who can help in professional growth.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register online for the expo.
  2. Receive registration confirmation by email.
  3. Visit the venue with college ID and resume.
  4. Attend workshops and speaker sessions.
  5. Meet companies and submit your resume.
  6. Build connections and apply for opportunities.
