Appointment Scheduling

  • Phone Scheduling: Call our clinic to speak with our staff and schedule an appointment.

Patient Instructions

  1. Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time.
  2. Bring any relevant medical records or test results to your appointment.
  3. Follow the clinic's COVID-19 safety protocols, including wearing a mask and maintaining social distancing.
  4. If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance.
  5. For emergencies, please call our clinic immediately or visit the nearest emergency room.

Appointment Process

  1. Patient requests an appointment through the online portal or by phone.
  2. Clinic staff reviews the request and confirms the appointment time with the patient.
  3. Patient arrives at the clinic for their scheduled appointment.
  4. Patient checks in at the reception desk and provides any necessary information.
  5. Patient is directed to the appropriate department for their consultation or treatment.
  6. After the appointment, patient receives any follow-up instructions or prescriptions as needed.
