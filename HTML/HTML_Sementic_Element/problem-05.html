National Career & Educational Fair 2026

Introduction

Welcome to the National Career & Education Fair 2026, a major platform designed to connect students, graduates, job seekers, educational institutions, recruiters and career experts. The fair provides opportunities to explore careers, internships, placements, scholarships, higher education programs and professional development options.

Participants can interact directly with organizations, attend career guidance sessions, learn about industry requirements and discover suitable educational and employment opportunities.

Organizer's Message:
Our goal is to help every participant discover the right career direction, connect with industry professionals and make informed decisions about their education and future.

Event Details

  • Event Name: National Career & Education Fair 2026
  • Date: 18-19 October 2026
  • Time: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Venue: India International Convention Centre,New Delhi
  • Entry:Free for registered participants
  • Audience:Students, graduates, job seekers and career explorers

Objective

The main objectives of the fair are:
  • Connect students with employers and educational institutions.
  • Provide information about career opportunities.
  • Help students discover internship and placement opportunities.
  • Introduce participants to scholarship programs.
  • Provide guidance about higher education.
  • Develop awareness of current industry skills.
  • Allow participants to interact with career experts.

Participant Guidance

Participants should register before attending the event. They should carry their student/college identification, a copy of their resume if applicable, and a list of questions about their career interests.

Visitors are encouraged to arrive early, attend relevant sessions, visit organization booths and communicate professionally with representatives.

Participating Organization

The fair will feature organizations from technology, finance, education, consulting, manufacturing and other sectors.
