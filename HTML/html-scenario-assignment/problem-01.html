About the College

GLA University is a premier institution of higher learning located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is known for its excellent academic programs and vibrant campus life.The university focuses on academic learning along with practical skills, research, innovation, extracurricular activities, and overall student development. Its campus covers approximately 110 acres and includes academic buildings, laboratories, residential facilities, sports facilities, and recreational spaces.
