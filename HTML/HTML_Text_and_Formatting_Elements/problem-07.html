What I Learned From My First Programming Experience

My first experience with programming taught me something important: getting stuck is part of learning. In the beginning, even simple errors could be confusing. A missing semicolon, an incorrect variable name, or a small logical mistake could stop the entire program from working. Instead of seeing errors as failures, I'm learning to treat them as clues. Debugging has become an important part of my learning process because it teaches me how to think about problems step by step. I still have a lot to learn, but I'm enjoying the process of turning an idea into something that actually works.
