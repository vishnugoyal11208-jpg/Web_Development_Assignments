About university

Welcome to the University Information Portal, a central source of information about our university, academic programs, departments, campus facilities, and important student information.Our university is committed to providing students with a strong academic foundation along with opportunities for practical learning, innovation, research, and personal development. The university encourages students to develop both technical knowledge and professional skills.Students are encouraged to participate in academic activities, workshops, seminars, competitions, and extracurricular programs. The university also promotes a learning environment where students can explore their interests and prepare for future careers.

Departments

The university has several academic departments that offer specialized education in different fields. Each department is supported by qualified faculty members and provides students with opportunities for theoretical and practical learning.

Department of Computer Science and Engineering focuses on programming, software development, computer networks, databases, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies.

Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering covers electronics, communication systems, embedded systems, signal processing, and related technologies.

Department of Mechanical Engineering provides education in areas such as manufacturing, thermodynamics, machine design, and industrial engineering.

Department of Civil Engineering focuses on construction, structural engineering, transportation, environmental engineering, and infrastructure development.

Academic Programs

The University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various disciplines. These programs are designed to provide students with both fundamental knowledge and practical skills.

The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) program provides students with a strong foundation in engineering and technology. Students can choose specializations according to their interests and career goals.

Students are expected to attend lectures regularly, complete assignments, participate in practical sessions, and appear for examinations as required by the university.

The academic year is generally divided into semesters. Each semester includes lectures, laboratory sessions, internal assessments, assignments, and end-semester examinations.

Campus Facilities

The university provides several facilities to support students in their academic and personal development.
The Central Library provides access to textbooks, reference materials, journals, newspapers, and digital learning resources. Students can use the library for academic research and self-study.
The Computer Laboratories provide computers and software required for programming, practical classes, and technical projects.
The campus also includes classrooms, laboratories, seminar halls, sports facilities, cafeterias, and common areas where students can study, collaborate, and participate in activities.
Students are expected to use campus facilities responsibly and maintain cleanliness and discipline.

Important Information

Students should regularly check official university notices for information about examinations, assignments, holidays, events, and academic schedules.

Attendance requirements must be followed according to university regulations.

Students should submit assignments before the specified deadlines. Late submission may result in penalties according to the applicable academic rules.
Student should carry their university identification card when required and follow all campus safety and disciplinary guidelines.
