About the College

GLA University is a premier institution of higher learning located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is known for its excellent academic programs and vibrant campus life.

Course Offered

The university offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs across various disciplines.
It offers diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, pharmacy , law, and sciences, holding an A+ grade from NAAC and 12B status from the UGC. 

Campus Life

The campus provides a vibrant and inclusive environment for students to grow academically and personally.
Students come from different states and countries, creating a multicultural community. The university maintains a focused, secure, and structured atmosphere with an emphasis on code of conduct and safety.
Celebrations include Diwali Mela, Diwali Night, Garba Night, and national holidays like Independence Day. Active groups focus on dance, drama, singing, fashion, art, and technical skill development.

Why I Like My College

It holds a NAAC 'A+' grade and UGC 12B status, which reflect its commitment to quality education and academic standards.
This university provides solid placement support, especially for Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) and IT branches, with recruiters like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and Amazon visiting the campus.
The 110-acre campus features modern academic blocks, well-equipped laboratories, a central library, and extensive sports facilities.
The institution supports vibrant student life with numerous technical and cultural festivals alongside a wide variety of active student clubs.
