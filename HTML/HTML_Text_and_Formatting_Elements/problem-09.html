Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1.Student must carry their college ID card while on campus.
2.Student must maintain cleanliness and take proper care of college property.
Student are expected to follow all instructions given by faculty members and college authorities.

Attendence

3. Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject.
4. Students must inform the concerned faculty member in case of an unavoidable absence.
requirements may vary according to institutional policies.

Examination

5. Students must carry their valid college ID card during examinations.
6. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the invigilator.
Late entry may not be permitted after the specified examination time.
Electronic devices such as mobile phones may not be allowed inside the examination hall.

Assignment Submission

7. Students must submit assignments before the specified deadline.
8. Assignments submitted after the deadline may receive a penalty or may not be accepted.
Example Deadline: Assignments must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the due date.
Students should keep a copy of their submitted assignments for future reference.

Discipline

9. Students must behave respectfully toward faculty, staff, and fellow students.
10. Bullying, harassment, disruptive behavior, and damage to college property are strictly prohibited.
Violation of disciplinary rules may result in appropriate action according to college policy.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for reading and following all academic and institutional rules.
Rules and deadlines may be updated by the institution when necessary.
