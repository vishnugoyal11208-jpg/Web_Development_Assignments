Innovate. Compete. Create. Inspire

The Annual College Technology Festival

Welcome to TECHNOVA 2026, an exciting college technology festival where creativity, innovation, coding, engineering, and technology come together. The festival provides students with an opportunity to showcase their technical skills, develop innovative ideas, participate in competitions, and learn from other technology enthusiasts.

Date: 15–16 October 2026
Venue: College Campus
Organized By: Department of Computer Science & Engineering
Theme: Building Tomorrow with Technology

Introduction

TECHNOVA gives students a platform to move beyond classroom learning and experiment with real-world technology.

Learn New Technologies

Participate in workshops and demonstrations covering areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Web Development, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and emerging technologies.

Showcase Your Innovation

Students can present their projects and ideas to judges, teachers, industry professionals, and fellow participants.

Compete With Other Students

Take part in exciting technical competitions and test your programming, designing, analytical, and engineering skills.

Build Your Network

Meet students from different colleges and departments

Earn Recognition

Winners and outstanding participants can receive certificates, trophies, prizes, and recognition for their achievements.

Highlights

1.CodeRush - Coding Competition

Test your programming and problem-solving skills in a challenging coding competition.

Participants will solve programming problems within a limited amount of time. Questions may involve algorithms, data structures, logical reasoning, and programming fundamentals.

Skills Tested: Programming, Logic, Algorithms, Problem Solving

2.HackNova - Hackathon

HackNova is an innovation challenge where teams work together to develop a technology-based solution to a real-world problem.

Participants can build prototypes or software solutions using their programming and technical knowledge.

Suggested Themes:

*Smart Education
*Artificial Intelligence
*Sustainable Technology
*Smart Cities
*Agriculture Technology
*Automation
Team Size: 2–4 members

3.RoboRace -- Robotics Challenge

RoboRace gives participants an opportunity to demonstrate their robotics and engineering skills.

Teams design and program robots that must successfully complete a specially designed track while overcoming different obstacles.

Skills Tested: Robotics, Electronics, Programming, Engineering Design
