Introduction

Aarohan University is a forward-looking institution committed to providing quality education, innovative learning experiences, and opportunities for students to develop the knowledge and skills needed for a changing world. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate programs across various disciplines, including science, arts, business, engineering, and technology. With a focus on holistic development, Aarohan University aims to nurture critical thinking, creativity, and leadership qualities in its students.

Our undergraduate programs combine classroom learning with practical projects, industry exposure, research opportunities, and student activities. With modern classrooms, well-equipped laboratories, libraries, sports facilities, and dedicated student support services, Aarohan University provides an environment where students can learn, grow, and build their future.

Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Computer Science
  • Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in Psychology
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)
  • Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Mechanical Engineering
  • Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) in Visual Arts

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the following general eligibility requirements:

  • Passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.
  • Mathematics and Physics are required for selected B.Tech. programs.
  • Applicants awaiting final Class 12 results may apply provisionally.
  • Program-specific eligibility requirements may vary.
  • Applicants should provide valid academic and identity documents during the admission process.
  • Minimum aggregate of 50% marks for general undergraduate programs.
