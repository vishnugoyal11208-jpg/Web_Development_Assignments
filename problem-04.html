Introduction to Quantum Physics

This course helps students learn the basics of Quantum Physics. Students will learn about light, atoms, waves & particles.

Course Objectives

  • Understand blackbody radiation.
  • Learn Planck's quantum theory.
  • Understand the photoelectric effect.
  • Learn about matter waves.
  • Study the uncertainty principle.
  • Know basic Schrödinger equation.

Learning Roadmap

  1. Learn modern physics basics.
  2. Study blackbody radiation.
  3. Learn quantum theory.
  4. Understand wave-particle duality.
  5. Complete quizzes and assignments.
