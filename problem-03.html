About Jaipur

Jaipur, popularly known as the Pink City, is the capital of Rajasthan and one of India's most famous tourist destinations. The city is known for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colourful markets, traditional handicrafts and rich cultural heritage.

Jaipur offers visitors a combination of history, architecture, food, shopping and traditional Rajasthani experiences. Famous landmarks such as Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and Jantar Mantar attract visitors from India and around the world.

Quick Facts:
  • Location: Rajasthan, India
  • Best season: October to March
  • Famous for: Forts, palaces, handicrafts and Rajasthani culture
  • Currency: Indian Rupee (₹)
  • Language: Hindi, English and Rajasthani
  • Time zone: IST (UTC+5:30)

Top Attraction in Jaipur

  • Amber Fort: Historic hilltop fort famous for its architecture.
  • Hawa Mahal: Famous five-storey palace with numerous windows.
  • City Palace: Royal complex containing courtyards, museums and palaces.
  • Jantar Mantar: Historic astronomical observatory.
  • Jal Mahal: Beautiful palace located in the middle of Man Sagar Lake.
  • Nahargarh Fort: Offers panoramic views of Jaipur.
  • Jaigarh Fort: Famous for its massive fortification and historic cannon.
  • Albert Hall Museum:Museum showcasing art and historical collections.

Travel Tips

  • Visit Jaipur between October and March for comfortable weather.
  • Carry sunscreen, sunglasses and a water bottle.
  • Wear comfortable shoes because many attractions require walking.
  • Start sightseeing early to avoid crowds and afternoon heat.
  • Respect local customs when visiting religious and historical sites.
  • Keep some cash available for local shops and transport.
  • Compare prices before buying souvenirs.
  • Stay hydrated while exploring outdoor attractions.
  • Book hotels and guided tours in advance during the peak tourist season.

Visitors Information

Check-in

Hotel check-in is generally available between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM, although timings may vary depending on the hotel.

Guided Tour

Visitors can book professional guides for forts, palaces, museums and heritage walks. A local guide can provide additional information about Jaipur's history and culture.

Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis, app-based cabs and local buses are available for travelling around Jaipur.

Travel Pass

Visitors should check the latest attraction combination tickets and transport options before travelling. Some attractions may offer combined entry tickets.
