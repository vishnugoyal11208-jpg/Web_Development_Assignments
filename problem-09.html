Welcome to Champion Sports Academy

Champion Sports Academy helps young athletes improve their skills in football, cricket, badminton, basketball & athletics.

We provide professional coaching, modern equipment and safe training facilities.

Visit our registration form to join the academy today.

Football Training Program

Football training includes passing, dribbling, shooting, fitness and team coordination.

Age Group: 10 to 18 years

Training Days: Monday, Wednesday and Friday

Cricket Training Program

Cricket coaching includes batting, bowling, fielding and match practice.

Age Group: 12 to 20 years

Training Days: Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday

Badminton Training Program

Learn serving, smashing, footwork and fitness with our experienced badminton coaches.

Age Group: 8 to 18 years
